User: What are the most common tree species in the Bronx?
SQL: SELECT spc_common, COUNT(*) AS count FROM trees WHERE geoid IN (SELECT tract_id FROM heat_debt_final WHERE borough = 'Bronx') GROUP BY spc_common ORDER BY count DESC LIMIT 10

User: What percentage of parking lots are in low-income areas?
